significance" statement was replaced accordingly.

## BrainGCN p-value reconciliation (k=10 family)

An independent recomputation (reviewer) gave 0.000929 / 0.001055 / 0.035156.
Diagnosis grid over {scipy tie-corrected, uncorrected} × {recomputed@0.5, CSV
`_cal` columns}: the MCC value 0.001055 equals this repo's convention exactly;
tie correction moves ROC 0.0318 ↔ 0.0401; the residual differences trace to
slightly different per-fold input scores. Every cell of the grid is p<0.05 on
all three metrics, so the conclusion is convention-independent. The manuscript
uses the convention that reproduces its own published k=9 family
(bal/MCC from saved probabilities at 0.5, stored per-fold ROC, scipy
tie-corrected Friedman): 0.0008 / 0.0011 / 0.032. The Methods (§2.7) now state
this tie-handling convention explicitly, and it is applied identically to the
primary nine-encoder family and the sensitivity family.
